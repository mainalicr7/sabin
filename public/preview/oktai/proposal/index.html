I work AI-first across design, code and motion, and I personally review every output before it reaches you. This is how I deliver in 2 weeks without cutting quality. It also mirrors what OKTAI stands for: AI does the heavy lifting, and a named person checks and signs off on the result.

03My understanding of the brief

  • The site has two parts: OKTAI Computer (the main focus) and CoPlanet (the company behind it). Both should feel like one experience.
  • The "computer" idea must be visible in the design, imagery and interactions.
  • The story follows the product flow: command center, goal, agents at work, validation, delivered result.
  • Custom OKTAI icons carry the story, step by step. They are not decoration.
  • Motion should match the quality, smoothness and pacing of your references, with its own OKTAI identity rather than a copy.
  • Your v19 content stays as written. Any change to wording or product claims goes to you for approval first.
  • The brand direction comes from your Brand Identity: the deep blue-violet primary with its secondary palette, dark gradient backgrounds, and an expert, calm, precise tone.

04Platform, technology and costs

Recommendation: custom code, custom design (no template)

  • Built with React components on Astro. Astro serves the pages as fast, lightweight HTML, which is ideal for speed and SEO. React powers the interactive and animated parts.
  • Motion: GSAP for scroll-driven animation and transitions, and Three.js (via React Three Fiber) for the 3D elements.
  • Two languages: English and German, each with its own URLs, page titles and descriptions. The structure allows more languages later (for example French and Italian) without a redesign.
  • Content editing: a headless CMS (Sanity) connected to the site, so your team can edit text and images in both languages without touching code. If you already use a CMS, I will build for that instead.
  • Hosting: Cloudflare Pages, which is fast worldwide and free for commercial sites. It can also run on your own hosting.

Why custom code instead of Framer or Webflow

  • Full control over motion: the 3D, scroll and interaction quality in your brief is not limited by a site builder's features.
  • No platform lock-in: you own all the code and can host it anywhere.
  • No monthly platform fees: no site-builder plan and no paid add-on per extra language.
  • Speed and SEO: fast pages by default, with clean markup and SEO settings for every language.

Recurring costs

  • Your domain, which you already have
  • Hosting and CMS run on free plans for a site of this size.

Paid fonts, plugins and services

  • None. Every library used is free and open source.
  • Fonts will be free fonts. The final choice is agreed with you during the design stage (your Brand Identity uses Poppins, which is free).
