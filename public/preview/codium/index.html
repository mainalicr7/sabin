Flat 2D art that moves like a real object

Your brief asks for 2D graphics that react to scroll, hover and click and look as 3D and lifelike as possible. Below are three small examples, one per trigger. Each one is a state machine: named states, clear inputs, and spring motion between them. The art here is my own, not from your designs.

1. Hover: depth and light

Move your pointer (or drag a finger) over the device. Layers sit at different depths, the light follows you and the shadow moves the other way.

state: idle

2. Scroll: an app comes apart

Keep scrolling. The stack opens layer by layer, labels arrive, and data starts moving between layers. Scroll back up and it closes again.

state: closed / progress 0%

3. Click: press, launch, land

Hover the button, then press it. The button squashes, the rocket fires, and the scene settles into a success state before it resets.

state: idle

How I would run it

  • Day 1-2: I go through your designs and list every animation with its trigger (scroll, hover, click), its states and its inputs. You approve that list, so we agree on the fixed scope before work starts.
  • Week 1: I build the 2 hardest animations first and send them as live previews, so you see the 3D feel early.
  • Week 2: the rest of the set, then 2 rounds of changes per animation.
  • Handover: .riv files, one state machine per illustration, and a short sheet for your developers with each input name (for example "hover" as a boolean, "scrollProgress" as a number from 0 to 100) and how to set it from JavaScript.

If Rive turns out to be the wrong fit for a piece (for example very heavy scroll scenes), I will say so early and suggest the option that works best for your build.

What I need from you

  • The design files with layers kept separate (Figma, Illustrator or SVG).
  • Any motion notes or reference sites you already have.
  • The page widths your site supports, so each animation is checked at phone and desktop size.
