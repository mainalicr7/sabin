Lake Natron: image picks

Pictures to sit behind the presenter for the Lake Natron section of a Mio green-screen video. Nine lines, a primary and a backup for each, in script order. All real photos or real satellite images, no AI, no watermarks.

Line by line

  1. 1. "This lake turns animals into statues."

    Fish eagle frozen on a branch like a stone statue. The hook.

    Primary: Nick Brandt, Petrified Fish Eagle, Lake Natron 2012. Backup: Petrified Songbird, 2010. Copyrighted, needs a license.
  2. 2. "Because it's full of natron,"

    Already vertical: the whole lake in red and pink streaks.

    Primary: NASA Earth Observatory, Landsat 8, public domain. Backup: ESA, Salty lakes, credit required.
  3. 3. "the salt the ancient Egyptians used"

    A real bag of natron from Tutankhamun's embalming cache. Clean museum shot.

    Primary: The Met, CC0. Backup: Natron deposits, Chad, CC BY-SA 4.0.
  4. 4. "to make mummies,"

    Gold and lapis coffin of Shoshenq II on black. Dramatic, no remains on show.

    Primary: Dale Cruse, CC BY 2.0. Backup: The Met, CC0.
  5. 5. "so anything that dies in it doesn't rot."

    A preserved flamingo on the lake, so the story turns toward flamingos.

    Primary: Nick Brandt, Petrified Flamingo, 2010. Backup: Petrified Dove, 2010. Copyrighted, needs a license.
  6. 6. "And yet nearly every flamingo in East Africa"

    A wall of pink lesser flamingos. Soft and bright.

    Primary: AlexanderMirschel, CC BY-SA 4.0. Backup: John Storr, Tanzania, public domain.
  7. 7. "is born here,"

    Fluffy chick on its mud nest beside a parent's pink leg. Stand-in: not shot at Natron.

    Primary: Eric Kilby, CC BY-SA 2.0. Backup: Vidhari911, CC BY 4.0.
  8. 8. "because nothing else can cross the water"

    Orange caustic water and white salt crust from above.

    Primary: NASA/METI ASTER, public domain. Backup: ESA, CC BY-SA 2.0.
  9. 9. "to reach the nests."

    Shot on Lake Natron itself: a dense pink colony with an airy white sky.

    Primary: Christoph Strassler, CC BY-SA 2.0. Backup: Liondartois, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Notes

  • The look moves cold to warm: grey statues and a gold coffin for the dark facts, then pink flamingos and red water for the twist.
  • Every primary is vertical or crops cleanly to 9:16, and the Commons files are 2,000 to 7,500 px on the long side, so they stay sharp full screen.
  • The two Nick Brandt photos are copyrighted fine art. Use them under a license from his studio, or swap in the backups.
  • CC BY and CC BY-SA pictures need a credit in the caption or on screen.
  • I could not find a freely licensed photo of chicks at Lake Natron itself, so line 7 uses a chick shot elsewhere.
