## Context

An email campaign produced a wave of responses. It was reported as a success in reaching new, cold leads.

## Problem

The report only looked at the campaign list. Nobody had checked whether the people who responded were already users.

## My role

I ran the attribution analysis and a list quality check on the same data.

## Approach

- I joined the responders against the full user base, not just the campaign list.
- I matched on cleaned email addresses, with typos and case differences handled.
- I checked the list for disposable email domains and for domains whose mail servers point to known throwaway providers. Standard blocklists miss many of these.

## Outcome

Most responders were existing users. The campaign was a successful win-back, not a cold lead win. That changed how its results were reported and changed the target of the next campaign. The list checks also removed low-quality addresses that would have hurt sending reputation.

## What I learned

Attribution depends on the join. Always compare results against everyone you already know, not only the list you sent to.
